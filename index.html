Dr. Momin practices classical homoeopathy with a focus on dermatology, and serves as a hospital administrator and medical teacher mentoring interns. Alongside his clinical work, he writes across medicine and self-development, and builds small tools like this one as a side interest.

About the sounds: every layer is synthesised live in your browser from filtered noise and oscillators, so the app needs no network and works fully offline. You can also add your own audio files as extra layers (More › Add sound files) — they are stored on your device and reused offline. The only optional download is the fast Opus encoder (More › Extras).
